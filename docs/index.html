Meet Pom Pom

Your air quality companion

A little dog on your keyring who can smell the air. When the air is clean, Pom Pom is full of beans. When it isn't, Pom Pom feels it too, and helps you find somewhere fresher.

Setting up (for grown-ups)

Pom Pom gets its air readings from AirTrack, so it's set up with the AirTrack app. It takes about five minutes, at home.

1

Get the app

Install AirTrack on your phone and sign in. Pom Pom needs an AirTrack Premium account: its sniffs come out of that account's monthly allowance.

2

Wake Pom Pom

Charge it, then press a button. The first time, its screen says Set me up and shows a QR code.

3

Scan the code

Point your phone's camera at the code. It opens the AirTrack app, which talks to Pom Pom for a moment over its own little Wi-Fi network.

4

Your Wi-Fi

Pick your home Wi-Fi in the app and type its password. You can add your phone's hotspot too, for answers on the go. When Pom Pom shows its face, you're done.

Start here

1

Sniff!

Press any button to wake Pom Pom, then the action button on the front to sniff the air. At home it tells you straight away what it smelt.

2

Go walkies

Press the menu button on the side, step down to Walkies and press the action button. A bag or a pocket is fine: it sniffs by itself as you go. (Why a bag is fine.)

3

Come home

Back on your Wi-Fi, Pom Pom works out the air at every sniff: "We're home! Press to see what I found!" Press for your trip, then today's chart.

4

Find clean air

Next time, try another way: the back streets, or through the park. Cleaner air makes Pom Pom happier, and brings rarer finds.

The two buttons

The action button

The big one on the front does things. On Pom Pom's face it sniffs the air, or once the bag has turned orange, shows your trip. Everywhere else it chooses, or goes to the next one.

The menu button

The little one on the side opens the menu and steps down it. From anything the menu opened, it takes you back. Keep it pressed for a second to go straight back to Pom Pom's face.

Not sure? The hint at the bottom of the screen always says what a press does right now.

What Pom Pom's face tells you

Along the top: Pom Pom's mood, the battery, and the air score from 0 to 100, where 100 is the cleanest. A little house means it's at home, a paw print means it's out and about. Pom Pom's mood always matches the last sniff.

Pom Pom never gets ill from the air for good, and it never blames you. When the air is poor it suggests somewhere fresher: a park, or a quieter street.

Sniff the air

Press the action button and Pom Pom's nose wrinkles while it sniffs. To work out the air, it needs to be on Wi-Fi.

At home, it tells you straight away, and its mood changes to match. Really clean air sets off the zoomies; poor air and it shakes it off.

Out and about there's usually no Wi-Fi, so Pom Pom remembers each sniff instead: the busy road, the park, the school gate. Back home it works them all out, and you can see how the air was along the way. If a grown-up has set up their phone's hotspot, Pom Pom can answer on the go too.

Press again within twenty seconds and Pom Pom gives the same answer without asking again. Each real sniff uses one of the month's sniffs, so there's no need to keep pressing.

How Pom Pom knows

Pom Pom isn't an air sensor: there's nothing inside that tests the air. It works out roughly where it is, and looks up the air there.

1

Where am I?

Pom Pom works out roughly where it is, close enough to know which street it's on.

2

What's the air like?

AirTrack looks up the air quality for that spot at that time: traffic, weather and the nearest monitoring stations, worked out street by street and updated every hour.

3

How does that feel?

The answer comes back as a score from 0 to 100, and Pom Pom's mood follows it.

So a bag is fine. Pom Pom doesn't need to breathe the air to know about it, so it works just as well zipped in a school bag or a pocket as clipped on the outside.
It's about streets, not steps. A busy road and a park can be very different, but the end of the garden won't differ from the back door. And smoke in the kitchen won't show up: Pom Pom knows the outdoor air.

Walkies

This is the big one. Out and about there's usually no Wi-Fi, so Pom Pom collects the air as you go, and shows you how it was when you get home.

Finds

Somewhere new, a sniff can turn up a find. Cleaner air means rarer finds, from a pebble to a butterfly. Nothing is ever taken away.

Coming home

Choose Home time in the menu when you're back. On your Wi-Fi, Pom Pom works out the air at every sniff and the bag turns orange.

Hunt for clean air. Orange blocks usually mean a busy road. Next time, try the back streets or the park, and see if your blocks turn greener.
Forgot to start walkies? If Pom Pom is carried for a few minutes it sets off by itself, and shows you next time you look. And if you forget Home time, it stops by itself after a while sitting still.

Play with Pom Pom

Pom Pom loves being treated gently. Banging or shaking it does nothing at all.

Now and then, when you wake Pom Pom, it says something about the air from its last sniff. Choose Tricks in the menu to see all its moves.

Looking after Pom Pom

Charge it every night

Like a phone. A charge lasts about a school day. The battery on the top strip turns amber under half and red when it's nearly empty.

Quiet at school

Sound starts off. To switch it on or off: press the menu button, then the action button on Sound.

If it gets stuck

Plug it into the charger. If it still doesn't wake up, ask the grown-up who gave it to you.

Privacy (for grown-ups)

Pom Pom gets its air readings from AirTrack, through the AirTrack account it was set up with. So it's covered by AirTrack's privacy policy and terms, just like the app.

What it sends

Only what it needs to look up the air: roughly where it is, and when. Nothing about who is carrying it: no names, no photos, no sound.

What it keeps

On the stick itself: its sniffs (when, the score and roughly where), its finds and the facts it has told. They stay on the stick.

The microphone

Only listens during Copycat. What it hears stays in memory for the game, and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Your account

Pom Pom has no account of its own. It uses your AirTrack Premium account, and its sniffs come out of that account's monthly allowance. Remove it under Connections in the AirTrack app's settings and it stops.
