# AirTrack Companion

Pom Pom is a small dog on a keychain whose mood follows the real air quality around it.
It's a pocket companion for children, built on an M5Stack StickS3 and the AirTrack API,
and made by [Air Aware](https://airaware.substack.com).

**[How to play with Pom Pom](https://air-aware.github.io/airtrack-companion/)**: the guide
for families.

The firmware and build instructions will be published here soon.
